💡 Help, Support & FAQs

Bidar City Municipal Corporation Grievance Redressal Guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take for a complaint to be resolved?

Resolution timelines depend on category SLAs: Streetlights & Sanitation (24-48 hours), Water Supply (72 hours), Road Repair (5 business days).

Q: What if the issue is marked resolved but still broken?

Click the "Reopen" button on the tracker within 7 days. This flags the case to the Municipal Commissioner for direct oversight.

Q: How do reminders work?

If a complaint breaches its target SLA, the system automatically fires escalation alerts to the department officer and the Commissioner.
